Brand identity

Everything we know about how She Drives looks and sounds, taken from the brand's own social graphics. Use it for the website, the rider and driver apps and the WhatsApp bot.

The name

She Drives

The wordmark is set in capitals with SHE in pink and DRIVES in ink. In sentences the brand writes itself as SheDrives, and the company as SheDrivesNG.

Wordmark

SHE DRIVES. Two colours, one weight, tight spacing.

In copy

SheDrives (the service), SheDrivesNG (the company, domain and handles).

Taglines

"Safe. Comfortable. Female driven." on light graphics, "Your Move, Our Priority" on photo graphics.

Logo

The sd monogram

A round monogram: a pink S wraps around a black d. It sits to the left of the wordmark, or alone inside a circle as a profile picture, on the steering wheel and on the car grille.

Type

Heavy, round, confident

A heavy geometric sans with round letters, close to Gilroy or Montserrat ExtraBold. Headlines mix ink and pink words in the same line. Montserrat is used here as a stand in until the designers confirm their font files.

Voice

Warm, direct, on her side

Short sentences. Speaks to one woman, about her day. Encourages without lecturing, and asks the audience questions.

"Move through Lagos city with confidence."

"Go where you need to go, when you need to go."

"Make this week the one you stop dreaming and start driving your own journey."

Do

  • Lead with safety and comfort, then convenience.
  • Say Lagos, say her plans, say her week.
  • Pick one word per headline to make pink.

Avoid

  • Fear based copy about men or danger.
  • Made up numbers, reviews or partners.
  • Long paragraphs on graphics.
