# Product summary (from the September 2026 platform proposal)

Full proposal: `SheDrivesNG_Development_Proposal.pdf`.

## Who it is for
- **Drivers:** female only.
- **Passengers:** in the full build all genders may book; each driver defaults to female passengers only
  and can opt in to all genders. Matching respects her setting.
- **Operations:** one admin portal; a government revenue portal.

## MVP (four months, one city, one ride category)
1. Ride booking: pickup, destination, immediate ride, driver accepts, trip status updates.
2. Verification: ID details and a selfie, reviewed by an admin.
3. Google Maps: places and travel-time estimates; a button opens Google Maps for navigation.
4. Fares from estimated time: minutes ÷ 60 × ₦20,000 (₦20,000 per hour; 30 min = ₦10,000). Shown before booking.
5. Driver details once assigned: name, photo, rating, vehicle, registration number.
6. In-app chat during an active booking.
7. Direct calling through the phone dialler.
8. Driver onboarding and inspection: details, documents, preferred inspection centre; admin records
   findings, rating, photos and report; driver gets status updates and the report.
9. Driver subscriptions: ₦20,000 weekly or ₦75,000 monthly, one payment gateway. No commission on rides.
   An active subscription is needed to receive bookings; expiry reminders.
10. Government revenue portal: ₦30 recorded per qualifying completed, paid booking; totals, statements,
    settlements entered by admins.
11. Admin portal: riders, drivers, verification, inspections, bookings, subscriptions, fares, revenue.
12. Two-way ratings.
13. Public website: the service, driver registration, contact information, app download links.
14. Notifications: in-app and push.

## Full build (months 5 to 9)
Automated face verification and liveness; passenger preferences; scheduled bookings; extended hire
(4-hour, daily, weekly); in-app navigation and internet calls; traffic overrun charges; VIP airport
pickups (₦80,000: ₦30,000 to SheDrivesNG, ₦50,000 to the driver); staff roles (inspection officer,
approving officer, support, auditor); trip PINs, journey sharing and incident reporting.

## Timeline
Month 1 design and foundations · Month 2 core ride experience · Month 3 operations and engagement ·
Month 4 pilot and launch · Months 5 to 8 full build · Month 9 validation and release.
