# Brand identity (extracted from the She Drives social graphics)

Source graphics: `brand/references/`.

## Name and taglines
- Wordmark: **SHE DRIVES** ("SHE" pink, "DRIVES" black). Written as SheDrives / SheDrivesNG in copy.
- Taglines used: "Safe. Comfortable. Female driven." and "Your Move, Our Priority".

## Logo
A circular monogram: a pink **S** wrapped around a black **d**. Sits left of the wordmark, or alone in a
circle (profile picture, steering wheel, car grille). A clean vector file is still needed from the team.

## Colour (sampled from the graphics)
| Role | Hex | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Brand pink | `#F5338B` | "SHE", key words ("confidence.", "NEW WEEK", "biggest"), buttons, icons |
| Deep pink | `#DD3B7D` | pressed/shadow side of the pink, gradients |
| Magenta night | `#990858` to `#580831` | dark photo overlays fading to near-black |
| Ink | `#1B1B1C` | "DRIVES", headlines, the "d" in the logo |
| Blush | `#FCE4F0` / `#F7EDF1` | light pink grounds, soft gradients |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | cards, speech bubbles, contact bar |

Pink is the hero; ink is the second voice; blush and white are the ground. Pink and ink are used together
in almost every headline.

## Type
Heavy geometric sans throughout (bold, tight, rounded counters), e.g. a Gilroy or Montserrat ExtraBold
feel. Headlines mix ink and pink words in the same line; body copy is a bold-ish sans. To confirm: the
exact font files the designers use.

## Visual motifs
- Speech and thought bubbles holding the headline.
- White rounded contact bar along the bottom (phone · web/Instagram · email), with pink circle icons.
- Pink-wrapped cars and a pink steering wheel with the monogram.
- Real Nigerian women at the wheel; warm, confident portraits.
- Curved swooshes and thin concentric arcs.

## Voice
Warm, confident, encouraging: "Move through Lagos city with confidence." · "Safe, reliable and convenient
rides for every woman on the move." · "Go where you need to go, when you need to go." Questions to the
audience ("What's your biggest transportation pet peeve?").
